About the founder

Rizichi Githinji is an apostolic-prophetic leader, ordained on June 19th, 2021, after a 40-year journey of hidden formation that began when she gave her life to Christ at the age of eight.

Her journey has been one of deep consecration, spiritual warfare, and alignment to God’s blueprint — a process that has shaped the pattern upon which Wellsprings 44 is built.

She carries a hybrid apostolic and prophetic mantle, with a mandate to raise and form prophetic people, establish altars and territories, build according to divine blueprints, and restore identity, sonship, and spiritual order.

The School of Prophets and the Prophetic was birthed out of a 40-day fast in 2022, where the Lord began to gather and form a people in what has been described as a “cave of Adullam” — a place of preparation, healing, and alignment.

About us

Preparing the Bride of Christ.

The mandate of Wellsprings 44 is to prepare the Bride of Christ for the Second Coming of Jesus Christ. This preparation involves forming believers who grow in spiritual maturity, discern the voice of God, walk in holiness, and serve faithfully within their God-given assignments. The work of Wellsprings 44 seeks to cultivate a community that reflects the character, wisdom, and authority of Christ in preparation for His return.

The vision of Wellsprings 44 is built upon four foundational pillars that support the life, formation, and mission of the community. These pillars work together to cultivate spiritual maturity, community life, and faithful service within the spheres where God has positioned His people.

🔷 The Pattern of Formation

God has always formed people in companies, not crowds. From Abraham to Jesus, we see a consistent pattern:

  • Abraham trained 318 in his house
  • Ruth was formed through covenant alignment
  • David raised mighty men in the cave
  • Gideon was refined to 300
  • Moses walked with the 70
  • Jesus formed the 12, sent the 70, and prepared the 120

This pattern reveals that transformation does not happen in isolation, but within intentional, governed formation environments. Wellsprings 44 stands within this pattern.

🔥 What Makes This Formation Distinct

Wellsprings 44 is not a course. It is a formation journey. This school was birthed through prayer, consecration, and a 40-day fast in 2022. What makes this school different is not just what is taught — it is how people are formed.

🔑 1. Formation, Not Information
Many programs focus on teaching content. Wellsprings 44 focuses on formation of the person including identity restoration, inner healing, deliverance, and character consecration.

🔑 2. A Biblical Pattern of Formation
God does not raise crowds — He forms companies. Wellsprings 44 exists to build such a company based on structural patterns found in Scripture.

🔑 3. Apostolic and Prophetic Foundation
Built on apostolic and prophetic foundations where structure meets Spirit, revelation meets governance, and calling meets assignment.

🔑 4. Integrated Formation (Spirit, Soul & Life)
The journey is holistic and intentional, balancing prophetic training with marketplace alignment and practical deployment elements.

🔑 5. A Governed and Accountable Environment
Not personality-driven. It is governed with proper apostolic oversight, formation tutors, and communal support metrics.

🔑 6. Activation, Not Observation
Students are participatory members who actively engage in assignments, prayer movements, and real-time application.

🔑 7. Commissioning, Not Just Completion
Graduation is not the end — it is the beginning of deployment into your strategic assignment.
